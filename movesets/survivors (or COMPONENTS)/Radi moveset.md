**Passive: Sharp eye

"Snazzy glasses, eh??"

Radi is able to see a star above the corrupted when Radi is atleast halfway into their terror radius (so like, layer 2 of chase??), informing him of their location.

**Q: Twirl

"Whats wrong, cant handle my intelligence??"

Radi, after a 0.3 second windup, will twirl his microphone staff infront of him. If a killer hits him while in this state, he'll gain **SPEED II** and apply **READ-ONLY** to the killer for 5 seconds, alongside a short 1 second stun that doesnt have any stun protection (due to how short it is), meaning one other survivor can add onto the stun. If he misses this block however, he'll be given **SHOCKED I** and **READ-ONLY** for 4 seconds. 34 second cooldown.

**E: grapple-line

"Swing and a MISS, my dear friend!"

after a 1 second windup, Radi will shoot his staff out, a string of dark magic shooting out of it. this string can travel forever, but will gradually lose its momentum and fall. when it hits a surface, after 0.5 seconds, Radi will be pulled to it, landing in a roll. if he's hit before the line lands/right as hes about to be pulled, the grapple will be cancelled and he'll be given **EXPLOITED** for 10 seconds and **MEMORY LEAK III** for 5. 55 second cooldown.

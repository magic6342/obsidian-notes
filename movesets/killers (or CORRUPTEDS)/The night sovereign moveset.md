The night sovereign


DIFFICULTY: 4/5 STARS



walk speed: 17 studs per second

run speed: 24 studs per second



DESCRIPTION: An alternate version of Eclipse, who succumbed to the control and influence that Shade possessed in their mind, now a puppet for the greater control.





QUOTE: **"AND YET, THE SOVEREIGN ROSE ONCE AGAIN."**





**Passive: silent step**

"D1DN'T YOU K-KNOW? GODS PREF3R THE *SILENCE*!!"

The night sovereign hovers a few feet above the floor, making his footsteps unheard. along with this, his terror radius is 25% smaller than all other killers.

**M1: heavenly jab**

"TH1S SP3AR MAY LOOK L1KE A SH4DOW, BUT 1T HURTS 4LL TH3 SAM3!!"

basic M1 attack, as per usual. deals 25 damage upon contact, with a 0.5 second windup and 1 second cooldown. also applies **PIRATED I** to the survivor.  

**Q: Blinding rush**

"C-C-C4TCH *THESE*, F4ILURE!"

The night sovereign will raise his spear toward the sky, summoning 3 holy orbs of magic, dampened and darkened by shadows. after a 1 second windup, the player can click on where to shoot the orbs- similar to !INSANITY's move. they have a small AOE upon contact with the floor, no AOE when hitting a player directly, and can travel for 4.5 seconds before exploding automatically. the AOE applies **BURNING** for 5 seconds, and knocks the player back a bit, also applying **READ-ONLY** for 5 seconds and damaging 10hp. direct contact will apply **BURNING** for 10 seconds and **READ-ONLY** for 7- dealing 15 damage. if directly hit, the same player cant be hit by any other orb during the attack (to stop them from dying from 3 orbs to the skull-). 60 second cooldown.

**E: JAVELIN**

"y-y0 bro i l34rn3d a new tr1ck, W-W-W4TCH TH1S!!"

The night sovereign slows down by 60%, raising one arm while holding their spear horizontally. after a 1.5 second windup, the night sovereign will follow it in a straight line following the camera (but no up/down movement, only horizontal). the spear curves to the nearest player, but not enough to just be a straight homing attack. if it hits, the survivor is given **SHOCKED II** and **MUTED**, as well as being dealt 30 damage. the spear will stick into the first wall it collides into, leaving the night sovereign to have to go physically pick it up (if a survivor is hit, the night sovereign will instantly be given the spear back). while spear-less, the night sovereign is unable to M1 and **BLINDING RUSH** will be weaker. 28 second cooldown + however long it takes to get the spear.

R: **RUSHDOWN**

"R34DY OR N0T, HER3 1 CO- n0, no, th4t's 2011X's l1n3..."

The night sovereign flys up a short distance, before flying diagonally down towards the ground, landing in a roll and speeding off at a high speed using their wings (probably around double his run speed? idk bruh), with turning thats... not sharp, i guess?? idk bro. When making contact with a survivor, the night sovereign will grab them, slashing them twice with their spear before grabbing them and spinning them around, then throwing them away. the survivor will be dealt 50 damage total (the total of 2 M1s) along with being given **MUTED** and **EXPLOITED**, landing in a ragdoll when thrown before getting up after 1.5 seconds. The night sovereign runs for 8 seconds total- when that 8 seconds is up, they'll skid to a half, planting their spear into the ground and ripping through said ground to slow themself down. If the night sovereign hits the 8 second timer and hasnt hit anybody (they stop upon hitting someone if that wasnt obvious), they'll be given **BLINDNESS III** and **READ-ONLY** for 5 seconds.

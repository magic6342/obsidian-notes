Passive: Plasma override

"\[T-T4K3-] (THAT, PUNK!)"

Metal protector MK.2 (whom will be referred to as metal protector from now) has a meter called "Plasma stability". every one of Metal Protector's moves fill this bar slowly, and when its full, the next 3 attacks will be plasma infused, making them stronger and apply SHOCKED II to the survivor for 5 seconds, at the cost of a longer cooldown and longer windup. plasma infused attacks can be interrupted by stuns, just like normal attacks, and will stun for longer.



M1: Claw swing

"so like, as you can see, this version has both hands- along with sharp, effective claws-" -a mysterious inventor.

Metal protector swings forward with one of their clawed hands, dealing 20 damage upon contact. can be held to shoot a short-ranged plasma blast, which will deal 5 extra damage and apply BURNED II for 5 seconds (2 dmg per second but a slight speed buff) to the survivor. 1.5 second cooldown (2 with plasma blast), and a 0.5 second windup.



Q: Long-shot

"isn't the plasma shot less effecti- OH, ok, nevermind-" -A spectator

Metal protector stops in place for 2 seconds, before lifting his left arm, aiming his shot. after a 0.3 second windup after clicking, he'll shoot a long-ranged beam of plasma, dealing 32 damage and applying READ-ONLY (locks you out of using abilities) to the survivor for 10 seconds, along with CORRUPTED II for 5 seconds- dealing a total of 37 damage (corrupted damages 1hp per second while also making popups appear that block your vision, which wont go away until closed). if you dont click to shoot after 3 seconds, Metal protector will lower his arm at the cost of needing to cool off- losing some of your plasma stability and becoming slower for 5 seconds. 20 second cooldown.



E: ignition

"ooohhh... cool wings!! wait why is it aiming its hand at m-" -A different (dead) spectator

Metal protector ignites it's wings, using them to hover up a small height. once at the peak, they stop, and after a 1.5 second windup, will full-speed fly and crash into where the player's camera is aimed, creating a shockwave and a small crater. direct hit applies 25 damage and DAZED II for 5 seconds. Being hit by the shockwave applies 10 damage and DAZED 1 for 3 seconds. easiest to use with shiftlock, as metal protector's exact location of landing is tied to where the centre of the camera is- and the shiftlock crosshair can help aim it. Has a long range, good for catching survivors off guard when in groups. 35 second cooldown.



R: Influenced eye

"\[1 C@N S33 Y0U H!D1NG.]"

simple detection ability. darkens screen, but will highlight all survivors and show their health. cooldown of 40 seconds, slows Metal protector down on use.

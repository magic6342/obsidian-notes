[[The night sovereign moveset]]

**SPEED:** increases speed by 10%, scales by 2 per level, capping at **SPEED III** (16% speed increase)



**BURNING**: damages 2hp per second while the survivor gets a small speedbuff.



**CORRUPTED**: 1hp damaged per second, pops up adverts and obstructive windows on the screen that need to be manually closed.



**READ-ONLY**: locks the survivor out of using their abilities for the specified duration.



**SHOCKED**: survivor becomes 10% slower (scales by 5% per status level, so **SHOCKED II** is 15%, etc.) and has a cooldown debuff of + 5 seconds to whatever the ability's cd is currently on for all abilities.



**404**: makes the survivor see in a dark grey and black monochromatic view for the specified duration. gets darker per level, stops at **404 III**.



**BLINDNESS**: blurs the survivors screen by 20%, scales by 10 per level, stops at **BLINDNESS III**.



**MUTED**: makes the survivor unable to hear terror radius and chase themes for the specified duration.



**EXPLOITED**: stops the player's cooldowns for all abilities for the specified duration- can be applied to both killers and survivors.



**PIRATED**: fills the player's screen with noise (as in the noise effect) and dampens all audio, both by 25% and scaling by 5% per level, stopping at **PIRATED VI** (65%)



**RANSOMED**: stops the player's movement and forces them to play a random minigame (wire connecting, matching shapes, typing the colour of a text (as in, text that's red that says blue)) in order to move again. upon completion, gives the player **SPEED II** for 3 seconds. minigames scale in number with each level, capping at only **RANSOMED II**, but could probably be increased further with private server commands.



**MEMORY LEAK**: deals 2 damage per second. with each level, the second increment goes down by 0.5- so **MEMORY LEAK II** deals 2 damage per 0.95 seconds (this is literally just bleeding from forsaken)



**PREVIOUS VERSION**: this status effect only has one level- when applied, after its duration (so, 5 seconds for example), the player will be teleported back to where they were when the effect started. the place they'll be teleported to is marked to them (client-sided) with an outline and a star. 



**HIDDEN:** makes the player 50% transparent for the specified duration. caps at **HIDDEN III** and scales by 8, meaning **HIDDEN III** will make the player 73% transparent.
